User Guide & Operational Manual

      
How to Use Vedic Mathematics Studio

      
      

        
1. Launching Directly Without PowerShell

        
Double-click Launch_Vedic_Math_Studio.bat or Launch_Vedic_Math_Studio.vbs on your Desktop to open the studio directly in your default browser.


        
2. Selecting Sutras & Customizing Inputs

        
Choose any of the 21+ Vedic methods from the left sidebar or the top "Select Sutra" dropdown. Type custom numbers into the input boxes (auto-calculated immediately) or click preset pills (e.g. 65², 98 × 97, 234 × 316).


        
3. Interacting with the Graph of the Equation

        
    
          
  • Pan: Click and drag the mouse across the canvas to explore the coordinate plane.
    • 
          
  • Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel or use + / − on the toolbar.
    • 
          
  • Reset View: Click ⟲ Reset to center all points of interest.
    • 
          
  • Toggles: Use 📐 Tangent to toggle tangent lines and 📍 Points to toggle roots, vertex, and coordinate labels.
    • 
          
  • Live HUD: Hover your mouse over the canvas to view real-time (x, y) mathematical coordinates.
    • 
        


        
4. Animated Step Playback

        
Use ▶ Play to automatically step through the calculations, or click ◀ and ▶ for single steps. You can also click directly on any step card in the right column to inspect that exact step.


        
5. Multi-Format Exports

        
Export your current problem report using the top header buttons: Word (.doc), PDF Document, Infographic (PNG), or Standalone Interactive HTML for complete offline portability.


        
6. Batch Verification Hub

        
Click "External Data Input Hub" in the sidebar to enter arbitrary CSV number pairs, run batch tests, and verify mathematical integrity via Beejank (Casting out 9s).
